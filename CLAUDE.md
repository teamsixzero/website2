# Sixzero

> Sixzero is a UX/UI design agency based in North Vancouver, BC, Canada, specializing in B2B SaaS and enterprise software. We help growing software companies build products people actually want to use through research-led design and senior UX talent.

Founded in 2017. 5.0 rating on Clutch.co. Serving clients across North America.

## Services

- **End-to-End UX/UI Design**: Project-based design engagements covering information architecture, wireframing, prototyping, and high-fidelity UI. Led by senior designers working closely with client teams.
- **UX Staff Augmentation**: On-demand senior UX designers and writers who integrate into existing product teams. Canadian-based, vetted for qualifications and cultural fit.

## Methodology

We follow a 5-step iterative process: Understand, Design, Test, Iterate, Repeat. Every engagement starts with talking to real users — not assumptions.

## Who We Work With

Software companies with complex workflows, mobile apps, and enterprise products. Primary contacts: Owners, CTOs, CMOs, Design Leaders, and Product Heads.

## What We Are Not

Not order takers. Not generalists. Not a pixel factory. We specialize exclusively in software product UX/UI — no print, no social media marketing, no junior talent.

## Notable Work

- PocketPills: Redesigned onboarding and ordering flows for Canada's largest online pharmacy
- Shop This City: Converted to PWA, grew users from 5k to 25k with 10x increase in active engagement
- SquadCast: Revamped cloud recording UI (acquired by Descript)
- Breakthrough Academy: Designed business coaching and staff management systems
- Asana and Handshake: Staff augmentation engagements
